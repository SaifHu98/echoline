# Store & Age Rating Certification Guidelines — ECHO//LINE (أصداء)

## 1. Age Rating Compliance

### GCAM (General Commission for Audiovisual Media - Saudi Arabia & MENA)
* **Rating Target**: Age 7+ / Everyone (General Audience).
* **Content Assessment**:
  * No blood, gore, violence, or sexual themes.
  * Cooperative puzzles centered on causal physics, logic, and historical restoration.
  * Moderated, localized semantic chat (zero toxicity / zero free-text profanity).
  * WebRTC voice chat disabled by default; requires explicit parental/account consent.

### PEGI & ESRB
* **PEGI Target**: PEGI 7 (Mild Fantasy Violence/Peril).
* **ESRB Target**: E10+ (Everyone 10+ - Fantasy Peril).
* **Interactive Elements**: Users Interact (In-game communication via localized pings & quick-messages).
